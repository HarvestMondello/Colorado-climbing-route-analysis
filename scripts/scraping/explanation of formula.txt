new .md to explain the Classic Score math (overview in the main page)

A classic route is has the consensus of the climbing community that it is of high quality, climbed a lot, loved enough to repeat, and widely agreed to be great.

A high Classic Score means a route is highly rated, frequently climbed, loved enough to repeat, and widely agreed to be great.

Formula:  all metrics are normalized
0.60(Quality)+0.15(Consensus)+0.15(Cult Following)+0.10(Popularity)
 
    # Composite score
    df["classic_score"] = (
        0.60 * df["norm_quality"]          # 60%
        + 0.15 * df["norm_consensus"]      # 15%
        + 0.15 * df["norm_cult_following"] # 15%
        + 0.10 * df["norm_popularity"]     # 10%
    )

⭐ Highly rated

👥 Frequently climbed

🔁 Loved enough to repeat

🤝 Widely agreed to be great


🧗 Quality: Star rating from Mountain Project (column: stars).

👥 Popularity: How many people climb it (from tick data: ticks_total, unique_climbers).

🔁 Cult Following: Repeat climbers — routes people come back to (from top_climber_*_ticks).

🤝 Consensus: How strong the agreement is about the rating (from stars and votes).
