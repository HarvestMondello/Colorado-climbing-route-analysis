## Overview  
- **Area:** Flatirons
- **Formation:** Second Flatiron
- **Grade:** YDS 5.0, 6 pitches
- **Length:** ~600'
- **Style:** Trad, multi pitch 
- **First Ascent:** n/a
- **Rock**: Fountain Formation sandstone

<br>

## Details

### Intro 
*Freeway on the Second Flatiron* is a classic. This is the most repeated route in Colorado. A **high-traffic classic**. This has a reputation is being easy and has a lot of accidents.   

### Route Characteristics  
- The East Face is tucked away in the trees giving it good winter and storm protection. 
- Easy technical climbing. 
 
### Approach & Descent 
- The approach is a steep hike starting by the trash can near a sign next to the Chautaqua Ranger Station.
- Freeway isn't a summit route and doesn't have a walk off. The route tops out at a tree. There are several other summit routes to the top.
