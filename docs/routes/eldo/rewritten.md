## Overview  
- **Area:**  Eldorado Canyon, Colorado
- **Formation:** Redgarden: Green Spur Butress
- **Grade:** YDS 5.7, 6 pitches
- **Route Length:** ~500'
- **Style:** Trad, multi-pich  
- **First Ascent:** George Hurley, Bob Culp, John Link, 1967
- **First Free Ascent** n/a
- **Rock type**: Foudntain Formation sandstone

<br>

## Details
### Intro 
A mega classic moderate. The most classic moderate in Eldo according to the dataset. The standard for this route is actually a linkup of The Great Zot pitch one (YDS 5.7 to 5.8+), Rewritten pitch 2-4 (YDS 5.7), Rebuffat's Arete (YDS 5.7) and Rewritten pitch 6. The original line is slightly easier but runout PG13 on P1 and not climbed very frequently. The first two pitches of Green Spur are often climbed as an approach to this route. The West Chimney to the Red Ledge is another approach route.

### Route Characteristics  
Varied climbing featuring a hand traverse (pitch 4) and steep crack and featured arete (Rebuffat's Arete)

### Approach & Descent 
Approach: The South Boulder Creek footbridge start to the Redgarden West trail.
Descent: Walk off, 3rd class and not described here.
