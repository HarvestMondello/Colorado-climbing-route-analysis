## Overview  
- **Area:** Eldorado Canyon, Colorado 
- **Formation:** The West Ridge: Long John Wall Tower
- **Grade:** YDS 5.8+, 4 pitches, II
- **Length:** ~400'
- **Style:** Trad, multi pitch
- **First Ascent:** Larry Dalke, Pat Ament, Wayne Goss, 1964
- **First Free Ascent** Larry Dalke, Pat Ament, Wayne Goss, 1964
- **Rock**: Fountain Formation sandstone

<br>

## Details
### Intro 
This varied and classic for the grade climb is very popular. 

### Route Characteristics  
Varied crack climbing with good gear. 

### Approach & Descent 
Approach: West South Boulder Creek footbridge via West Ridge climbers trail.
Descent: Standard is the Redgarden trail to the Boulder creek footbridge (4th walk off to get there). Some will rapel Sooberg - watch for climbers on route.

## Seasonality
### Seasonal Profile & Sun Aspect  
- **Seasonality:** Year round acess.
- **Sun Exposure:** Southwest facing. Early morning shade on the lower portions of the route (pitch 1-2). Sun the rest of the day.
