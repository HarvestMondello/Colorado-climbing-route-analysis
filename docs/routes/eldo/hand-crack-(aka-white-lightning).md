## Overview  
- **Area:** Eldorado Canyon, Colorado 
- **Formation:** The West Ridge
- **Grade:** YDS 5.10A, 1 pitches
- **Length:** 80' / 24m
- **Style:** Trad, single pitch
- **First Ascent:** na
- **First Free Ascent** na
- **Rock**: Fountain Formation sandstone

<br>

## Details
### Intro 
This short and sweet crack packs a lot of crack variety.

### Route Characteristics  
Variety of crack climbing from fingers to hands.

### Approach & Descent 
Approach: West South Boulder Creek footbridge via West Ridge climbers trail.
Descent: Single rap. Reverse approach.
