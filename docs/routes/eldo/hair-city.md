- **Route Length:** na
- **Style:** Trad, multi-pitch  
- **First Ascent:** Jim Erickson, John Behrens, Stand Badgett, 1969
- **First Free Ascent** Jim Erickson, John Behrens, Stand Badgett, 1969	
- **Rock type**: Fountain Formation sandstone

## Details
### Intro 
A mental testpiece for the grade due to head run-outs.

### Route Characteristics  
Steep technical face climbing followed by a roof.

### Approach & Descent 
Apprach: From South Boulder Creek footbridge to Bastille climbers trail.
Descent: Standard Bastille walk off, 4th class downclimb.
