## Overview  
- **Area:**  Eldorado Canyon, Colorado
- **Formation:** Wind Tower: Southwest Face
- **Grade:** YDS 5.7, 3-4 pitches (depending on variation)
- **Length:** ~250'
- **Style:** Trad, multi-pitch
- **First Ascent:** Layton Kor and Jane Bendixon, 1959
- **Rock**: Fountain Formation sandstone

<br>

## Details
### Intro 


### Route Characteristics  
Classic hand crack with varied climbing. 

### Approach & Descent 
Approach: Uphill from the South Boulder Creek footbridge.
Descent: Rapel or downclimb (4th)
