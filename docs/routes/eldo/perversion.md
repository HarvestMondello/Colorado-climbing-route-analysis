## Overview  
- **Area:** OSMP Wildlife Preserve, adjacent to Eldorado Canyon, Colorado 
- **Formation:** Mickey Mouse Wall, Eldorado Mountain
- **Grade:** YDS 5.9, 3 pitches, II.
- **Length:** ~300'
- **Style:** Trad, multi pitch
- **First Ascent:** Layton Kor, Bob Culp, George Hurley and others, 1963
- **First Free Ascent** 
- **Rock**: Fountain Formation sandstone

<br>

## Details
### Intro 
A hidden classic with a relatively long approach and seasonal nesting closures. 

### Route Characteristics  
Stellar finger cracks, corner climbing and a handcrack.

### Approach & Descent 
Apprach: County Road 67 to climber's trail. DO NOT trespass on the railroad tracks which is private property. 
Descent: Rapels (not described here).
