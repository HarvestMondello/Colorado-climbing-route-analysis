## Overview  
- **Area:** Cathedral Spires, South Platte
- **Formation:** Cynical Pinnacle
- **Grade:** 5.9, 3 pitches, II
- **Length:** ~300'
- **Style:** Trad, multi pitch
- **First Ascent:**  Bill Roos, Paul Sibley, Carl Arndt, and Bernum Arndt, 1968 
- **Rock**: Granite

<br>

## Details
### Route Characteristics  
- Center Route is considered by many to be the finest 5.9 crack climb in the state of Colorado. Pitch one is a 5.9 finger crack, pitch two a splitter 5.9+ hand crack and pitch three is wider and easier. There is an alternate P3 that's a 5.10A crack with finger crack crux. Considered one of the best 5.9 crack climbs in the State of Colorado.

### Approach & Descent 
Approach: Uphill hike. 
Descent: Four rappels.
